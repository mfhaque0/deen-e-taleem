
> **In the name of Allah, the Most Beneficent and Merciful**

> “Wasting time is worse than death. When you die, you’re simply being cut off from this world and the people in it. But when you waste time, you cut yourself off from God and the Hereafter.”
>
> — Ibn al-Qayyim رحمه الله
>
> **Reference:** Al-Fawā'id by Ibn al-Qayyim  
> https://www.kalamullah.com/Books/Al-Fawaid-Imam-Ibn-Qayyim-al-Jawziyyah.pdf

These profound words of Imam Ibn al-Qayyim رحمه الله force us to reflect upon one of the greatest blessings Allah has bestowed upon us: time.

Most people fear death because it marks the end of their connection to this world. Yet Ibn al-Qayyim points our attention to something even more dangerous than death itself: wasting the precious moments Allah has granted us.

Death separates a person from this temporary world. Wasting time separates a person from Allah, from worship, from repentance, from righteous deeds, and from preparation for the eternal life of the Hereafter.

Allah Himself swears by time in the Qur'an:

> "By the ˹passage of˺ time!
>
> Surely humanity is in ˹grave˺ loss,
>
> except those who have faith, do good, and urge each other to the truth, and urge each other to perseverance."
>
> **(Surah Al-'Asr 103:1-3)**
>
> **Reference:** https://quran.com/103

Every passing second is bringing us closer to our meeting with Allah. Every moment that leaves us is a moment that can never be recovered. Allah informs us that every human being is in loss except those who spend their lives in faith, righteous deeds, truth, and patience.

---

## We Were Created for a Purpose

Allah says:

> "Did you then think that We had created you without purpose, and that you would never be returned to Us?"
>
> **(Surah Al-Mu'minun 23:115)**
>
> **Reference:** https://quran.com/23/115

Not only does Allah tell us that our lives have a purpose, He tells us exactly what that purpose is:

> "I did not create jinn and humans except to worship Me."
>
> **(Surah Adh-Dhariyat 51:56)**
>
> **Reference:** https://quran.com/51/56

Our existence is not a coincidence. We were not created merely to eat, sleep, work, accumulate wealth, and entertain ourselves. We were created to worship Allah and prepare for our return to Him.

Every day, every hour, and every minute is an opportunity to fulfill that purpose. When time is wasted, opportunities for worship, repentance, seeking knowledge, helping others, and earning Allah's pleasure are lost forever.

---

## Allah Sees How We Spend Every Moment

Allah says:

> "He is the One Who created the heavens and the earth in six Days, then established Himself on the Throne. He knows whatever goes into the earth and whatever comes out of it, and whatever descends from the sky and whatever ascends into it. And He is with you wherever you are. For Allah is All-Seeing of what you do."
>
> **(Surah Al-Hadid 57:4)**
>
> **Reference:** https://quran.com/57/4

Every moment of our lives is known to Allah. Every prayer, every act of charity, every page of Qur'an recited, every beneficial deed, and every moment spent in heedlessness is recorded.

Nothing escapes His knowledge.

---

## The Things That Distract Us

Allah says:

> "O believers! Do not let your wealth or your children distract you from the remembrance of Allah. Whoever does so, it is they who are the true losers."
>
> **(Surah Al-Munafiqun 63:9)**
>
> **Reference:** https://quran.com/63/9

Many people lose sight of their purpose because they become consumed by worldly distractions. Wealth, entertainment, social status, careers, and endless pursuits can occupy a person's life until years pass without meaningful progress toward Allah.

Never before in history has wasting time been easier. Endless scrolling, entertainment, and distractions can consume hours without a person realizing how much of his life has passed.

A believer constantly asks himself:

**Will this matter when I stand before Allah?**

---

## The Regret of Those Who Wasted Their Lives

One of the most painful scenes in the Qur'an is the regret of those who delayed repentance and righteous deeds until death arrived.

Allah says:

> "When death approaches any of them, they cry, 'My Lord! Let me go back,
>
> so I may do good in what I left behind.' Never! It is only a ˹useless˺ appeal they make. And there is a barrier behind them until the Day they are resurrected."
>
> **(Surah Al-Mu'minun 23:99-100)**
>
> **Reference:** https://quran.com/23/99-100

How many people plan to repent tomorrow?

How many plan to seek knowledge later?

How many intend to become better Muslims later?

Yet death often arrives before "later" ever comes.

---

## How Short Life Really Is

Allah says:

> "The Day they see it, it will be as though they had stayed in the world no more than an evening or its morning."
>
> **(Surah An-Nazi'at 79:46)**
>
> **Reference:** https://quran.com/79/46

No matter how long a person lives, worldly life will seem incredibly short on the Day of Judgment.

Decades will feel like a single evening.

The opportunities we ignored and the moments we wasted will suddenly appear more valuable than anything we once chased.

---

## Two Blessings Many People Waste

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:

> "There are two blessings that many people are deceived into losing: health and free time."
>
> **(Sahih al-Bukhari 6412)**
>
> **Reference:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6412

Health and free time are among the greatest blessings Allah grants His servants. Unfortunately, many people only recognize their value after they are gone.

---

## Take Advantage Before It Is Too Late

Ibn Abbas reported:

> The Messenger of Allah, peace and blessings be upon him, said,
>
> "Take advantage of five before five: your youth before your old age, your health before your illness, your riches before your poverty, your free time before your work, and your life before your death."
>
> **(Shu'ab al-Iman 10250 — Sahih according to Al-Albani)**
>
> **Reference:** https://shamela.ws/book/9983/10075

Youth eventually becomes old age.

Health is replaced by illness.

Wealth may become poverty.

Free time becomes occupied.

Life eventually ends in death.

The wise believer benefits from these blessings before they disappear.

---

## Every Moment Will Be Questioned

Abu Barzah Al-Aslami narrated that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:

> "The feet of the slave of Allah shall not move [on the Day of Judgement] until he is asked about five things: about his life and what he did with it, about his knowledge and what he did with it, about his wealth and how he earned it and where he spent it on, about his body and for what did he wear it out."
>
> **(Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2417)**
>
> **Reference:** https://sunnah.com/tirmidhi:2417

Every hour spent will be accounted for.

Every day will be questioned.

Every blessing will be examined.

Nothing will be forgotten.

---

## Live Like a Traveler

Narrated Mujahid:

> `Abdullah bin `Umar said,
>
> "Allah's Messenger (ﷺ) took hold of my shoulder and said, 'Be in this world as if you were a stranger or a traveler.'"
>
> The sub-narrator added:
>
> Ibn `Umar used to say,
>
> "If you survive till the evening, do not expect to be alive in the morning, and if you survive till the morning, do not expect to be alive in the evening, and take from your health for your sickness, and (take) from your life for your death."
>
> **(Sahih al-Bukhari 6416)**
>
> **Reference:** https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6416

A traveler never mistakes the road for the destination.

Likewise, the believer remembers that this world is temporary and that the Hereafter is the true home.

---

## When Death Comes, Deeds End

Abu Huraira (Allah be pleased with him) reported Allah's Messenger ﷺ as saying:

> "When a man dies, his acts come to an end, but three, recurring charity, or knowledge (by which people) benefit, or a pious child, who prays for him (for the deceased)."
>
> **(Sahih Muslim 1631)**
>
> **Reference:** https://sunnah.com/muslim:1631a

Every moment before death is therefore priceless.

Every sincere prayer.

Every act of charity.

Every beneficial lesson taught.

Every verse recited.

Every righteous deed performed for the sake of Allah.

All of these may become a source of reward long after we have left this world.

---

## A Question for Ourselves

Before closing this article, ask yourself:

- What is taking most of my time?
- Is it bringing me closer to Allah or further away from Him?
- If death came today, would I be satisfied with how I spent this week?
- What deed can I start today that will benefit me after I die?

The best time to change is now.

---

## Conclusion

The reality is simple.

Death cuts us off from the world.

Wasted time cuts us off from Allah and the Hereafter.

The clock never stops.

Days become weeks.

Weeks become months.

Months become years.

Years become a lifetime.

The wise believer does not merely count the days of his life; he strives to make his days count.

As Ibn al-Qayyim رحمه الله said:

> “Wasting time is worse than death. When you die, you’re simply being cut off from this world and the people in it. But when you waste time, you cut yourself off from God and the Hereafter.”

May Allah bless our time, help us use it in ways that please Him, protect us from heedlessness, and allow us to meet Him with lives filled with faith and righteous deeds.

**Ameen.**
