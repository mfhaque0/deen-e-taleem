
### Navigating the Battle Against Temptation: A Guide to Faith and Resilience

![](https://cdn-images-1.medium.com/max/800/1*Uqnm0WxN7l0nGJ8SZU_rlg.jpeg)

In the name of Allah, the Most Beneficent and Merciful

-   **_The Battle Against Temptation_**

In the middle of an exciting movie, you find yourself caught up in a beautiful scene. As you feel good, Shaytan starts whispering tempting thoughts in your ear, leading you toward sinful actions. You pause the movie, but then you end up watching inappropriate content, starting a habit that began with what seemed like a harmless moment. This is a common struggle many face, and it’s important to realize that these situations often come from wanting **pleasure, greed, lust and desire.**

-   **_Repentance (توبة)_**

When faced with temptations, it’s essential to remember the power of dua and engage in sincere tawbah (repentance). Continuously ask Allah for help to avoid sins. If your repentance is genuine, Allah will provide you with the strength to resist them. Repentance involves sincerely seeking forgiveness from Allah and making a genuine effort not to repeat the sin. Whenever the urge to sin arises, express your commitment by saying “**مَعَاذَ اللَّهِ.”** Invoking Allah’s name creates a protective barrier against temptation, empowering you to overcome your desires.

-   **_Seeking Allah’s Help_**

Don’t succumb to Shaytan’s whispers. While avoiding sins may feel challenging initially, patience and self-control will eventually lead you to peace through righteous actions. Allah always assists those who strive to draw closer to Him. Seeking Allah’s help is essential for overcoming temptation. By turning to Him in sincere prayer (dua), we acknowledge our limitations and rely on His guidance. Regular acts of worship, such as Salah (prayer) and recitation of the Quran, strengthen our connection with Allah.

We often make the same mistakes repeatedly, and in the back of our minds, we may think, “In two weeks, I’ll be doing this again.” **However, tawbah is not for those who persist in their evil deeds; such individuals have accepted their path**. It’s vital to commit seriously to change and seek support from trusted friends and family members when the struggle feels overwhelming.

-   **_Staying Aware of Allah_**

As your desire intensifies, Shaytan’s whispers grow louder. In those moments, remember that Allah is watching over you. When faced with temptation, allow that fear to guide you rather than suppress it. Say to yourself, “**Allah is watching me**.” This reminder can help steer you away from sin.

Take inspiration from the story of Yusuf (AS). When he was in testing, and the wife of Al Aziz came and wanted to tempt him and make him fall into sin, and she was alone with him in the room. His first words were, “مَعَاذَ اللَّهِ” (May Allah protect us). This powerful statement illustrates how we can seek refuge in Allah when faced with temptation.

-   **_Fear of Allah’s Knowledge_**

Don’t ignore the feeling of fear in your heart; instead, articulate it. Allah reminds us in Surah An-Nisa ([4:108](https://quran.com/4?startingVerse=108)): “**They try to hide ˹their deception˺ from people, but they can never hide it from Allah — in Whose presence they plot by night what is displeasing to Him. And Allah is Fully Aware of what they do**.” This verse serves as a poignant reminder that while humans may hide their wrongdoings from one another, nothing escapes Allah’s gaze.

-   **_The Importance of Good Deeds_**

The Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said, “**(The following are) two words (sentences or utterances) that are very easy for the tongue to say, and very heavy in the balance (of reward), and most beloved to the Gracious Almighty: Subhan Allahi wa bi-hamdihi; Subhan Allahi-l-’Adhim”** [Sahih al-Bukhari 6682](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:6682).

**Indeed, nothing is heavier when it’s weighted with the name of Allah.**

**Your Lord says: “Call on Me; I will answer your (Prayer): but those who are too arrogant to serve Me will surely find themselves in Hell — in humiliation!**” (Ghafir [40:60](https://myislam.org/surah-ghafir/ayat-60/)). Remembering Allah and engaging in acts of worship can fortify your resolve. “**Surely good deeds wipe out evil deeds**” (Quran [11:114](https://quran.com/11?startingVerse=114)), emphasizing the cleansing power of righteous actions.

Furthermore, Allah instructs: “**Indeed, prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing, and the remembrance of Allah is greater. And Allah knows that which you do**” (Quran [29:45](https://myislam.org/surah-ankabut/ayat-45/)). This underscores the importance of maintaining a close relationship with Allah through prayer and remembrance.

One of my favourite quote of Bilal ibn Sa’d, he advises: “**_Do not look at the smallness of the sin, rather look at the greatness of the one whom you disobeyed, the Almighty_**.” When you think of Allah’s greatness, it can empower you to lower your gaze and resist inappropriate content, enabling you to dismiss the evil thoughts that cross your mind.

-   **_Understanding the Impact of Sin_**

Engaging with inappropriate content deprives you of the sweetness of Imaan. It weakens your heart and soul, making you less interested in worship and drawing closer to Allah. As stated in “**For indeed the soul is ever inclined to evil, except those shown mercy by my Lord**” (Quran [12:53](https://quran.com/en/yusuf/53)), we must recognize the intrinsic struggle against our desires.

As you navigate this journey, remember, "**_Fear for yourself before people fear for you and weep for your sins before people weep for you_**” (Bilal ibn Sa’d). Too often, we get caught in a cycle of desire, lust, pleasure, and temptation. **To achieve true success, focus on long-term happiness rather than fleeting pleasure**.

This requires self-discipline, mental strength, and firm **tawakkul** (trust) in Allah. In the end, you need a strong reason or goal to stay firm in this struggle.

-   **_Conclusion_**

_In conclusion, the battle against temptation is a continuous journey that requires vigilance, support, and an unwavering commitment to your faith. By seeking refuge in Allah, practicing sincere repentance, and engaging in acts of worship, you can fortify your spirit against the whispers of Shaytan and find solace in the righteous path._
